Demo walkthrough

The end-to-end story, in order. Every link opens the live page backed by the database. Sign in as admin to see all steps.

  1. The school is a registered, eligible beneficiary. Beneficiaries
  2. A 7-day nutrition programme with meal plans exists and the school is enrolled. Programmes
  3. Calculate the total food required for the week (open the programme, then "Total food required"). Programmes
  4. Check inventory: batches, expiry dates, quality status, low stock and expiring items. Stock | Alerts | Batches
  5. Raise a procurement request for the shortage (from the distribution preview), approve it, create a purchase order and receive it: a new batch appears in stock. New plan | Requests | Orders
  6. Create the distribution plan (stock is reserved first-expiry-first), dispatch it, then confirm delivery. Distribution
  7. Record a quality inspection that quarantines a batch, a wastage event and a corrective action. Inspection | Wastage | Actions
  8. The dashboard shows the updated stock, procurement, distribution and wastage figures. Dashboard | Reports
